Panduan Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

A. SOP Cara Sanggah TJPH
  1. Tarik data suite TJPH, lalu pindahkan ke sheet TJPH.
  2. Buat folder Google Drive berisi screenshot FMS & UNSLOG, serta bukti CCTV inbound dan outbound.
  3. Atur izin akses Google Drive ke email SPX & Shopee resmi.
  4. Salin link Google Drive ke sheet TJPH, lalu isi alasan sanggah sesuai status paket.
  5. Isi Web Form 3PL (Ops, Transit Point Serang DC, Case TJPH, Sanggah Claim).
  6. Kirim email pemberitahuan ke tim terkait.
B. SOP Data Gantungan Hourly
  1. Buka FMS > Order Management > Order Tracking.
  2. Pilih status: SOC_RECEIVED, SOC_PACKED, SOC_PACKING (dan status RETURN_*), salin ID paket.
  3. Isi sheet Data Gantungan Hourly: Tanggal, SPX TN, Order Status, Current Station, Next Station.
C. SOP MCC FMHub_LHTransported
  1. FMS > Order Tracking, pilih status FMHub_LHTransported, salin ID.
  2. Isi sheet MCC: date, SPX TN, Current TO Number, Current & Next Station, Last Status, remarks.
  3. Kirim feedback email berisi alasan sanggah, screenshot pivot table, dan file attachment MCC.
D. SOP Proses NCR (Non-Conformance Report)
  1. Isi sheet SJM Transit Point Serang DC to Cakung DC.
  2. Isi template Non-AWB & SPX Liquidation Pre-Alert Form (Tujuan: Jakarta Return DC).
  3. Isi Copy of NCR Template, siapkan foto driver & foto fisik barang.
  4. Kirim email SJM, upload foto barang & lampirkan sheet SJM.
E. SOP Proses RTS (Return to Sender) & Cancel Paket
  1. RTS: Perangkat PDA > Intra-Station Handover > Create Task > WS IHT Desentral Fixed_EHA > EHA Outbound.
  2. Paket Cancel: PDA IHT In > FMS Exception Handling Area > EHA Single Inbound > Resolve > Print AWB > IHT Out.
  3. Catat di sheet Monitoring Tracert EHA & EHA Reporting.
F. SOP Damage (Kerusakan) & Repacking
  1. Ambil foto/video inbound & unboxing, info FM di grup SeaTalk.
  2. Buat BA Damage, email beserta foto/video (ZIP).
  3. Isi Web Damage Handling & FMS EHA Single Inbound > Eskalasi ke Team Claim.
  4. Repacking: Proses PDA EHA In, lakban ulang rapi, lalu IHT EHA Outbound.
